### Credential status definitions

- **Owner-reported; evidence pending:** The portfolio owner reports the credential or record, but the public credential page/certificate has not been added to this repository.
- **In progress:** Active enrollment or ongoing study is confirmed.
- **Completed — evidence pending:** Completion is confirmed by the learner, but verification details are not recorded.
- **Verified:** Exact title, issuer, date where available, and official verification URL have been checked against the issuer's record.
- **Anticipated:** A future academic milestone; not a completed degree or credential.

## 3. Credential verification workflow

For each item, record:
1. Exact title as displayed by the issuing organization.
2. Issuer and delivery platform (distinguish HarvardX/edX from Harvard University executive education, for example).
3. Completion date and credential ID, if provided.
4. Official share/verification URL.
5. A project artifact that demonstrates application.

**CS50 note:** CS50 distinguishes its free CS50 certificate from an edX verified certificate. The certificate type should be identified accurately; do not label a course certificate “edX verified” unless the edX credential itself confirms that status. See [CS50AI certificate FAQ](https://cs50.harvard.edu/ai/faqs/).

## 4. Application evidence standard

A credential should link to an artifact that demonstrates application—not merely mention a project. Useful evidence includes:
- Problem statement, audience, and requirements.
- Acceptance criteria and decision rationale.
- Implementation or reproducible analysis.
- Tests/evaluation, limitations, and risk controls.
- Measured outcome with baseline, method, and period—or a clearly labeled hypothesis if not measured.
